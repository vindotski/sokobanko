# Sokoban's components

The components behind [Sokoban](https://sokoban.game), each in its states, in light and dark.

- **UI** are the shadcn-vue primitives in `app/components/ui`, styled with the tokens in `app/assets/css/main.css`: square corners, no shadows, black on white (or white on black), 44px controls.
- **App** are Sokoban's own components: level previews and cards, the level switcher, the play page's menus and camera controls, and the PixiJS board itself.
- **Design** shows the tokens: colours, type, sizing and the board's pieces, drawn from the live CSS variables.

Switch light and dark with the **Theme** toolbar, and phone or desktop with **Viewport**.

## Every story is a test

`pnpm test:storybook` renders every story in headless Chromium, in light and then dark (the projects `storybook-light` and `storybook-dark` in `vitest.config.ts`). Each story must:

- **render** without errors;
- **pass its play function**, if it has one: clicks, keys and the events they emit;
- **pass axe** (the Accessibility panel): contrast, names, roles. A violation fails CI;
- **match its screenshot** in `__screenshots__/`, on CI's Linux. A story opts out with `parameters: { visual: false }`.

Stories live in `stories/` (not next to the components, where Nuxt would register them as components). The [Development page](https://github.com/vindotski/sokoban/blob/main/docs/Development.md#storybook) has how to write them and update the screenshots.
